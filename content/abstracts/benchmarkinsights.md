### Abstract

Benchmarking studies are critical for assessing method performance. This workshop is for researchers who want to learn different ways of visualising and comparing method performance after running a benchmarking study. It will use the benchmarkInsights module from our R package BenchHub that provides a series of functions to visualise benchmarking results. The session will begin with a brief background on benchmarking metrics, followed by a hands-on session where participants will:
- interact with benchmarkInsights class
- generate summary visualisation
- inspect method performance across methods and datasets
- identify robust methods
